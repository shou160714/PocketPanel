FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What do I need to use PocketPanel?

You need the PocketPanel desktop agent on your PC (Windows, with macOS planned) and the PocketPanel mobile app on your Android or iOS device.

Does PocketPanel collect any personal data or require an account?

No. PocketPanel does not require user accounts and never collects or transmits personal information, telemetry, or crash reports.

Can I connect over VPN when I'm away from home?

Yes. QR pairing stores both your local Wi-Fi and VPN (e.g., Tailscale) addresses and automatically falls back when you switch networks.
