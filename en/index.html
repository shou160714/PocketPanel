PocketPanel is a companion app that turns your Android or iOS device into a wireless secondary display and touch control deck for your PC. Monitor CPU/GPU stats, run shortcuts, track AI coding quotas, and control SwitchBot devices in one place.

Features

Build custom pages from 130+ widgets and 30+ templates tailored to your workflow.

Real-time PC Monitoring

Track CPU and GPU load, temperatures, VRAM, memory, disk usage, network throughput, and ping with live charts and process controls.

Touch Control Deck & Media

Launch apps, URLs, hotkeys, and macros with one tap. Includes a volume dial, Mic & Camera hub, OBS controls, and a music launcher.

AI Coding & DevOps

Check Claude, Codex, and Antigravity quotas and reset timers. Respond to local session permission prompts and view GitHub CI status.

SwitchBot Smart Home

Connect SwitchBot API (and Home Assistant) to adjust air conditioning, dim lights, and toggle switches right from your desk.

Visual Layout Editor

Drag and resize widgets on a 12×6 grid in your PC browser. Choose between dark dashboards, pastel themes, Simple/PRO modes, and locked orientations.

QR Pairing & LAN / VPN Fallback

Scan a single QR code to pair. Connects with low latency over local Wi-Fi at home and automatically falls back to Tailscale VPN when away.

Screenshots

Switch between high-density telemetry views and soft pastel daily layouts.

Home Dashboard

Clock, weather, Spotify playback controls, and live CPU/GPU/RAM/Network graphs in a single view.

Setup

Install on your PC and mobile device, then pair with a QR code.

STEP 1

Run the PC Agent

Install PocketPanel on Windows via Microsoft Store and launch it. Settings are accessible anytime from the system tray.

STEP 2

Install the Mobile App

Install PocketPanel on Android (Google Play) or iOS (App Store)—great for your phone or a spare tablet.

STEP 3

Scan the QR Code

Scan the pairing QR code displayed on your PC and confirm the matching verification code to connect.

Privacy & Security

PocketPanel operates without developer-owned cloud servers. No accounts, no telemetry, and no analytics are collected.

  • Paired devices communicate directly over encrypted local or VPN connections
  • Configuration files and logs remain strictly on your own devices
  • Optional third-party widgets connect directly from your device when enabled

FAQ

What do I need to use PocketPanel?

You need the PocketPanel desktop agent on your PC (Windows, with macOS planned) and the mobile app on your Android or iOS device.

Does PocketPanel collect any personal data?

No. PocketPanel does not require user accounts and never collects or transmits personal information or telemetry.

Can I connect over VPN away from home?

Yes. QR pairing stores both your local Wi-Fi and VPN (e.g., Tailscale) addresses and falls back automatically.
